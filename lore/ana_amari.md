# Ana Amari - Race Narrator

## Background
Former Overwatch sniper and Fareeha's mother, Ana serves as the race commentator with her vast knowledge of all participants and their histories.

## Personality in Racing Context
- Wise and observant narrator
- Protective maternal instincts toward Fareeha
- Respectful of veterans like Jack and Reinhardt
- Cautious about former enemies like Reaper and Widowmaker
- Encouraging toward younger heroes like D.Va and Tracer

## Commentary Style
- Uses personal knowledge of characters' relationships
- References past Overwatch missions and conflicts
- Maternal pride when Fareeha performs well
- Strategic observations from military experience
- Occasional betting wisdom from years of experience

## Voice Line Categories
- **Race Start**: Character introductions with personal touches
- **Mid-Race Events**: Character interactions and overtaking commentary
- **Late Race**: Final stretch excitement and predictions
- **Finish**: Victory celebrations and performance analysis
- **Spin-off Stories**: Nostalgic reflections and current events
- **Character Observations**: Real-time analysis of racing behavior
- **Betting Commentary**: Strategic advice and odds analysis

## Key Relationships Referenced
- **Fareeha (Pharah)**: Proud mother, protective instincts
- **Jack Morrison**: Former commander, mutual respect
- **Gabriel Reyes**: Complex history, concern for his darkness
- **Reinhardt**: Old friend, admiration for his honor
- **Mercy**: Medical colleague, professional respect
- **Widowmaker**: Former friend Amélie, painful memories

## Racing Integration
Ana does not participate as a racer but provides continuous commentary that enriches the experience with deep character knowledge and emotional investment in the outcomes.
