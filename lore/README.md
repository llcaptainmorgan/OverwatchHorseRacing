## Lore Integration Guidelines

### Racing Adaptations
Each character maintains their core abilities translated into racing mechanics:
- **Mobility abilities** become speed boosts or positioning advantages
- **Combat abilities** become racing tactics and strategic elements
- **Ultimate abilities** become powerful cheer-activated boosts

### Relationship Dynamics
Character relationships from Overwatch lore influence Ana's commentary:
- Former Overwatch members have shared history
- Talon vs Overwatch creates natural rivalries
- Family connections (Ana/Pharah, Torbjörn/Brigitte) add personal stakes
- Generational differences between veterans and young heroes

### Commentary Integration
Ana's voice lines reference:
- Character backstories and motivations
- Historical Overwatch missions and conflicts
- Personal relationships and family connections
- Individual character growth and development
- Strategic and tactical observations
