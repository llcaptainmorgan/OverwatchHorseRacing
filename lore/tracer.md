# Lena "Tracer" Oxton - Racing Profile

## Character Overview
Former Overwatch pilot with chronal acceleration technology, transformed into the fastest and most unpredictable racer on the track.

## Racing Form: Chrono-Enhanced Speedster
- Orange and blue racing gear with chronal accelerator visible
- Goggles adapted for high-speed racing
- Chronal anchor device prevents temporal displacement during races
- Distinctive spiky brown hair flows in speed trails

## Racing Characteristics
- **Type**: Pure Speed/Burst Runner
- **Strengths**: Fastest acceleration, blink ability for instant position changes
- **Weaknesses**: Less consistent than steady runners, can overextend
- **Special Ability**: Chronal blink for short-distance teleportation

## Personality Traits
- Cheerful and optimistic
- Loves the thrill of competition
- Friendly rivalry with all racers
- Never gives up, always pushing forward

## Ana's Commentary Notes
- Described as "playful zip" and "unpredictable pace"
- Often mentioned in context of speed vs strategy
- Ana shows maternal concern for her young enthusiasm
- Frequently used in comparisons for pure speed

## Racing Strategy
- Explosive start with chronal acceleration
- Uses blink strategically to avoid traffic
- Maintains high speed but can be inconsistent
- Benefits greatly from crowd energy and cheers

## Cheer Boost Effects
- **Single Cheer**: Chronal accelerator boost
- **Multiple Cheers**: Blink recharge speed increase
- **Spam Penalty**: Temporal displacement, confusion effects

## Victory Quotes
- "Cheers, love! The cavalry's here!"
- "That was brilliant!"
- "Easy peasy!"

## Key Rivalries/Relationships
- **D.Va**: Friendly speed competition
- **Soldier: 76**: Respectful of veteran experience
- **Widowmaker**: Cautious rivalry due to Overwatch history
- **Winston**: Scientific admiration for chronal technology

## Technical Notes
- Chronal accelerator requires careful calibration for racing
- Blink ability has cooldown periods during race
- Time dilation effects create visual spectacle during acceleration
- Temporal anchor prevents race timeline disruption
