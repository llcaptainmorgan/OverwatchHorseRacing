# Fareeha "Pharah" Amari - Racing Profile

## Character Overview
Elite security chief turned rocket-powered centaur racer, carrying the legacy of her mother Ana Amari and the Overwatch organization.

## Racing Form: Rocket-Powered Centaur
- Blue and gold armor plating
- Rocket thrusters integrated into hindquarters
- Combat helmet with targeting visor
- Egyptian-inspired design elements

## Racing Characteristics
- **Type**: Balanced Power Runner
- **Strengths**: Strong mid-race performance, consistent speed
- **Weaknesses**: Can be overtaken by pure speed characters
- **Special Ability**: Rocket boost for short bursts

## Personality Traits
- Disciplined and focused
- Strong sense of justice
- Protective of teammates
- Driven to prove herself worthy of the Amari name

## Ana's Commentary Notes
- Source of maternal pride
- Often mentioned with protective undertones
- Compared favorably to Ana's own military precision
- Referenced in context of "new generation" vs veterans

## Racing Strategy
- Maintains steady pace early race
- Uses rocket boost strategically in mid-race
- Strong finisher when properly supported by crowd cheers
- Benefits from tactical positioning

## Cheer Boost Effects
- **Single Cheer**: Minor speed increase
- **Multiple Cheers**: Rocket boost activation
- **Spam Penalty**: Overheated thrusters, speed reduction

## Victory Quotes
- "Justice rains from above!"
- "For honor and glory!"
- "The Amari name lives on!"

## Key Rivalries/Relationships
- **Ana**: Proud mother cheering from commentary booth
- **Soldier: 76**: Mutual respect as soldiers
- **Mercy**: Professional admiration
- **Widowmaker**: Cautious rivalry due to Ana's history
